Canelo Álvarez

Saúl Álvarez Barragán is a Mexican boxer from Guadalajara, world champion in four weight classes and one of the biggest stars in boxing history.

About

Short biography

Saúl Álvarez was born on July 18, 1990 in Guadalajara, Jalisco, and grew up in Juanacatlán, a small town. He comes from a big family of boxers and started boxing around age 13, following his older brothers. He turned professional at just 15 years old and became one of the most successful Mexican fighters ever.

Important facts

  • Age: 36. Height: about 1.71 m.
  • Professional record: 63 wins, 3 losses, 2 draws (39 KOs).
  • His three losses: Floyd Mayweather (2013), Dmitry Bivol (2022) and Terence Crawford (2025).
  • Trainer: Eddy Reynoso.
  • Next fight: Christian Mbilli, October 31, 2026 in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, for the WBC super middleweight title.

Career highlights

  • Debuted as a professional on October 29, 2005, at age 15.
  • Became a world champion at light middleweight, middleweight, super middleweight and light heavyweight.
  • Became the undisputed super middleweight champion in November 2021.
  • Lost to Terence Crawford in September 2025 and had elbow surgery in October 2025; he is now coming back.

Works (Important Fights)

1. vs. Floyd Mayweather (September 14, 2013)

His first professional loss. Mayweather won by majority decision in a huge fight, but Canelo learned from it and came back stronger.

2. vs. Gennady Golovkin I and II (2017 and 2018)

Their first fight in September 2017 ended in a controversial draw. In the rematch a year later, Canelo won by majority decision. It is one of the greatest rivalries of this era.

3. vs. Sergey Kovalev (November 2, 2019)

In his light heavyweight debut, Canelo knocked out former champion Kovalev in round 11 and won the WBO title, making him a champion in a fourth weight class.

4. vs. Caleb Plant (November 6, 2021)

Canelo stopped Plant in round 11 in Las Vegas and became the undisputed super middleweight champion, holding all four major belts.
